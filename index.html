The IBC promises a 330-day resolution timeline. In practice, cases at NCLT take an average of 597 days for the CIRP itself (IBBI Mar 2025) — and the plan-approval stage alone is currently delayed by up to 738 days, according to the Supreme Court's suo motu order of April 2026. Of the cases that close, more end in liquidation than in resolution.

What this dashboard is

A public, free, open-data view of NCLT — built for everyone who needs to understand the system.

✓Systemic, not case-level

We track how the institution is performing in aggregate. We do not list individual cases or send cause-list alerts — specialist services exist for that.

✓Free and open

No login, no subscription, no paywall. Every dataset downloadable as CSV under a Creative Commons licence so researchers and journalists can build on it.

✓Verifiable methodology

Every figure carries a source and a date. Source PDFs are archived. Derivation formulas are published. Researchers can replicate our numbers.

✓Policy-grade analysis

The Research hub provides comparative, historical, and statutory context — how NCLT measures against its own promise and against international peers.
